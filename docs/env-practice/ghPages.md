# Viewing Your Game on GitHub Pages

The gba-scenic environment automatically publishes your games online so anyone can play them in their browser without needing to install anything! We'll see how that works here.

## Viewing Deployments

1. Go to your GitHub repo for `env-learning`. Make sure your username is at the top of the screen.
1. On the right hand side of the screen there should be a section labeled "Deployments". You may need to scroll down to find it.
1. Click on **github-pages** under Deployments.
1. Here you will be able to see links to the deployed version of your game site. If you very recently pushed changes, it may not be done deploying. Once it is deployed, you can click the link to view your page.
1. Congratulations! You have your first game published for the world to see. You can play it on the computer, or even on your phone just by visiting the `github.io` URL!
